Privacy policy

Claude Workspace (personal)

  • Who uses it: only me, with my own Google accounts.
  • What it accesses: only the Google data in accounts I've signed in to myself (Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Calendar), and only when I ask.
  • Where the data goes: requests go straight from my computer to Google. Nothing is collected or kept on any other server, sold, or shared with anyone.
  • The assistant: the assistant's conversations are handled by Anthropic under its own privacy policy.
  • Sending email: it cannot send email.
  • Stored sign-ins: the sign-in tokens are kept only on my own computer. I can revoke them at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Contact: github.com/Mookahfr.
